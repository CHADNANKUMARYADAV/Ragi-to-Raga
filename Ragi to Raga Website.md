## Eat clean. Feel light.

A village-style cafe hidden off busy Kanakapura Road, just a short walk from Doddakallasandra Metro. Part of the Shankaraa Foundation, our kitchen brings back traditional recipes made the way they used to be.

Pure

### No maida, no additives

No refined flour, artificial colours or soda. Millets, rice and fresh local produce instead.

Calm

### Garden seating

Shaded open-air tables under trees, indoor seating, books and a small play area for kids.

Local

### Karnataka classics

Akki roti, paddu, neer dosa and ottu shavige, with a few modern surprises.

Together

### Music and merchandise

Evening concerts now and then, and a small merchandise corner to take a little of us home.
